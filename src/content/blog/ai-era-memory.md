People will remember this period for the models. That part is obvious.

What I pay attention to more quietly is the tone: how we talk while we build. Are we honest about limits? Do we get drunk on demos? Do we leave room for the people who will live with these systems after the launch posts fade?

I don’t have a manifesto. I have a few preferences I try to keep:

- be clear about what a system can and can’t do
- stay curious without turning every advance into theater
- ask who is helped, and who might be ignored

I’m not above getting excited. I get excited all the time. I just don’t want excitement to become a substitute for care.

If AI keeps moving into everyday life, and it will, then patience, clarity, and ordinary kindness aren’t side skills. They’re part of the work. I’d rather be remembered as someone who took that seriously than as someone who only chased the next impressive number.
