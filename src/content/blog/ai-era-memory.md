People will remember this era for the models, of course. The demos. The leaps. The strange new tools that make old tasks feel newly light.

I hope they also remember the tone we chose while building them.

Did we talk about intelligence like conquerors, or like caretakers? Did we leave room for wonder without drowning in hype? Did we stay kind to the people whose lives these systems touch?

I don’t have a grand manifesto. I have a preference:

Build carefully. Speak honestly. Stay curious. Keep your humanity louder than your personal brand.

If AI becomes a bigger part of everyday life — and it will — then the soft skills are not soft at all. Patience, clarity, empathy, and the courage to say “slow down” are part of the craft.

I want to be the kind of engineer who can love the frontier and still ask, gently: *who does this help, and who does this overlook?*

That question will never trend as hard as a benchmark. It might matter more.
